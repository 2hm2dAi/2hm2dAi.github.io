Privacy Policy

Octopus is a personal app used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

It accesses the owner's Google Calendar and Google Tasks only to show summaries and manage the owner's own events and tasks.

Data is processed on the owner's private server. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
